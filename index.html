03 · Little things that help

Practical travel tips

  • Transport: check whether a T-money or other transport card suits your routes; keep a little cash for top-ups if needed.
  • Maps: save hotel addresses in Korean and download offline map areas before you leave.
  • Connectivity: arrange an eSIM, SIM or roaming plan and keep your hotel address accessible offline.
  • Money: carry more than one payment method and separate backup cash from your wallet.
  • Etiquette: check queueing, dining and temple/palace rules; remove shoes where signs or hosts ask.
  • Winter: use thermal layers, insulated gloves, warm socks, a beanie, lip balm and shoes with grip. Vivaldi Park can feel colder than Seoul.
  • Snow & ice: walk slowly, watch for slippery pavement and leave extra travel time during snowfall.
  • Food: mention allergies clearly; use Papago to translate dietary needs and confirm ingredients with staff.
  • Bookings: recheck holiday opening hours and reservation confirmations, especially around Christmas and New Year.
  • Safety: share your itinerary with someone you trust and keep emergency contacts and travel insurance details offline.
